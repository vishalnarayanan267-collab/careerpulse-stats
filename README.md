# CareerPulse

## Tagline

> Turning graduate employment data into actionable institutional intelligence.

## 📌 Overview

CareerPulse is an **Employment Outcomes Intelligence Dashboard** built for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 — SIH26135: Employment Outcomes Tracking**.

Institutions collect large amounts of graduate and placement data, but raw spreadsheets alone do not provide an easy way to understand employment trends, department-level gaps, or factors associated with better outcomes.

CareerPulse transforms structured graduate data into an interactive analytics experience where users can:

- Upload graduate employment data through CSV
- Validate and preview the dataset
- Automatically calculate employment KPIs
- Analyze outcomes across departments and graduation years
- Explore individual graduate records
- Identify areas that need attention
- Generate rule-based recommendations from the data

The goal is simple:

**Raw Data → Analytics → Insights → Better Decisions**

## ❗ Problem Statement

### SIH26135 — Employment Outcomes Tracking

Institutions need to understand what happens to their graduates after graduation.

Relevant information can include:

- Graduate details
- Department
- Graduation year
- CGPA
- Internship experience
- Employment status
- Company
- Job role
- Industry
- Location
- Salary
- Time to employment
- Degree relevance
- Higher studies

As this information grows across multiple cohorts, manually analyzing spreadsheets becomes increasingly difficult.

The challenge is therefore not only **collecting employment data**, but converting that data into information that institutions can actually use.
